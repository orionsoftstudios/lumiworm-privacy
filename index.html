Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

Lumiworm is a game by Orion Soft Games. You don't need an account, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and that is the only way data leaves your phone.

What we collect

We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and we don't run servers that store player data. Your rank, coins, characters and settings are saved only on your device.

Advertising

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can also read how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Lumiworm is intended for players aged 13 and older.

Deleting your data

You can erase your progress under Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game. We hold no copy of it. To ask about data collected for ads, see Google's policy linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
